Told You Need an Annual Backflow Test?

If a letter from the city or your property manager mentioned backflow testing and you're not sure what that means, it's an annual check on the device that keeps contaminated water from siphoning back into Westminster's drinking water supply, and most homes and businesses with irrigation systems or certain fixtures are required to have one.

What a Backflow Preventer Actually Does

A backflow preventer stops water from flowing backward into the clean supply line if pressure drops unexpectedly, which matters most on irrigation systems, commercial fixtures, and any connection where contaminated water could otherwise be pulled back into the drinking supply.

Testing confirms the device's check valves are actually holding under pressure, since a preventer can fail silently without any visible sign until it's tested.

Who Actually Needs This

Homes with irrigation systems connected to the municipal supply, commercial properties, and any property Westy Water, the City of Westminster's own Public Works and Utilities department or the city has flagged for a required device all need annual testing, typically performed by a certified tester and reported directly to the utility.

Filing the Test Report

Once testing is complete, we file the required report directly with the utility on your behalf, which is the step that actually satisfies the annual requirement, not just having the test performed.

If a device fails testing, we quote the repair or replacement on the spot so the property isn't left out of compliance any longer than necessary.

Beyond the Annual Requirement

A backflow preventer that fails testing isn't just a compliance issue, it's a real gap in the protection between your plumbing and the municipal supply, which matters most on properties with chemical irrigation systems or commercial connections.

We check the device's overall condition during testing, not just whether it technically passes, and flag anything trending toward failure before it actually does.

Scheduling Your Annual Test

We can set a standing annual reminder so testing happens before your compliance deadline each year, rather than leaving it to chance or a notice from the utility.

A standard test takes about 20 to 30 minutes per device, and we file the report with the utility the same day in most cases.

Devices We Test and Install

We work with the reduced-pressure zone and double-check valve assemblies most commonly required on residential irrigation and commercial connections in Westminster, along with pressure vacuum breakers on simpler setups.

If your device is aging or was installed before current standards, we'll tell you plainly whether it's worth repairing versus replacing outright rather than patching something that's likely to fail its next annual test anyway.

Serving Westminster and Nearby

Backflow testing requests come primarily from irrigation-equipped homes and commercial properties in 80023 and 80234, though we test anywhere in the city on request.

A missed annual deadline is easy to lose track of on a busy property, and we're glad to send a courtesy reminder the month before yours is due if you'd rather not track it yourself.

Common Questions

Backflow Testing & Prevention FAQ

Is backflow testing required in Westminster?

Properties with an irrigation system tied to the municipal water supply, along with most commercial connections, are generally required to have an annual test reported to the utility. We handle the testing and file the report directly.

What happens if I skip the annual test?

Depending on the utility's enforcement, a property can face a notice, a fine, or in some cases a water service restriction for an unresolved required test. It's a small annual cost to avoid a bigger headache.
