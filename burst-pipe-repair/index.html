Shut the Water Off, Then Call

A burst pipe is one of the clearest true emergencies in plumbing. Shut the water off at the main immediately, then call, since every minute of delay is more water into the floor, walls, or ceiling.

Westminster's Front Range winters run a freeze-thaw pattern rather than one long deep freeze, and that repeated cycle is what actually cracks pipe, not a single cold snap.

What Causes Most Burst Pipes Here

Frozen pipe that's expanded and cracked during a hard freeze is the most common cause in Westminster winters, followed by aging galvanized supply lines that have corroded thin enough to fail under normal pressure.

A burst near a water heater or washing machine connection is often a failed supply line hose rather than the home's main plumbing, which is a much simpler and cheaper fix.

After the Immediate Repair

Once the burst section is repaired and water is restored, we check nearby fittings and connections for stress damage from the pressure event, since a burst in one spot occasionally strains a connection elsewhere on the same line.

If the damage reached drywall, flooring, or insulation, that's a separate conversation about water damage restoration, which we can coordinate directly rather than leaving you to find a second contractor.

What Determines Repair Cost Most

Access is the biggest factor, a burst pipe in an open basement ceiling is a faster, cheaper repair than the same break inside a finished wall that needs to be opened and later patched.

Pipe material also matters. PEX is generally faster and cheaper to repair than copper or older galvanized sections, which sometimes need a broader section replaced rather than a single spot fix.

What to Do While You Wait

Shut off the main, open the nearest faucet to relieve pressure, and move anything valuable away from the affected area if it's safe to do so while waiting for us to arrive.

We prioritize active burst pipe calls ahead of scheduled work, since ongoing flooding is a different level of urgency than a repair that can safely wait a few hours.

Materials We Repair With

We match the repair material to what's already in the wall where reasonable, PEX to PEX, copper to copper, rather than defaulting to whatever's fastest, since mismatched materials can create their own corrosion issues down the line.

For an older galvanized section that's already failing, we'll flag it as a candidate for a larger repipe conversation rather than just patching the same weak point again.

Serving Westminster and Nearby

Burst pipe emergencies happen citywide during a hard freeze, though older exposed-pipe housing near 80030 and 80221 sees them more often than newer, better-insulated construction.

Once the immediate crisis is handled, we'll walk the rest of the home's exposed piping with you if you'd like, since a burst in one spot is sometimes an early warning for a second weak point elsewhere on the same system.

Signs You Shouldn't Ignore

  • Water actively spraying or flowing from a pipe
  • Sudden loss of water pressure with a visible leak
  • Water stains spreading quickly on a wall or ceiling
  • A hissing or rushing sound inside a wall
  • Flooding near an exterior wall after a hard freeze

Common Questions

Burst Pipe Repair FAQ

Where's my main shutoff valve?

Most Westminster homes have the main shutoff in the basement, a crawl space, or along an exterior wall near where the water line enters the house. If you're not sure, it's worth locating it before an emergency happens, not during one.

Should I turn off the whole house's water or just one section?

If you're not sure which section feeds the burst pipe, shutting off the main is the safer call. We can help you identify individual shutoffs for next time once the immediate emergency is handled.
