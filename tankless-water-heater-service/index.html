Running Out of Hot Water With No Tank in Sight?

If your Westminster home has an on-demand unit and the water is running lukewarm, cutting out, or scaling faster than it used to, you're describing what plumbers call tankless water heater service, not a full replacement. Most of these calls are a flush, a descale, or a sensor issue rather than a dead unit.

Why Tankless Units Need Their Own Service Routine

A tankless water heater doesn't fail the way a tank does. Instead of slowly rusting out, it scales up on the inside of its heat exchanger, and Most of Westminster's tap water arrives via Westy Water from Clear Creek near Golden, and that supply runs harder than a lot of newcomers expect coming from softer-water states. hard water speeds that process considerably.

Left unflushed, mineral buildup restricts flow through the narrow passages the unit depends on, which is what actually causes the two most common complaints: water that starts hot and fades, and a unit that short-cycles between temperatures.

What a Real Service Visit Covers

A proper visit isolates the unit with its service valves, runs a descaling solution through a closed loop for roughly 45 minutes to an hour, and flushes the lines clean before checking the inlet filter and igniter.

We also pull the error code history off the unit's display, since most tankless brands log the exact fault rather than leaving a technician guessing.

What Actually Drives the Price

Brand and BTU output matter less to the final price than access. A unit mounted in an easily reached utility closet is a faster job than one tucked behind finished drywall in a remodel, and that labor difference shows up in the quote more than the part itself does.

A unit under warranty may only need the labor covered out of pocket, which is worth checking with the manufacturer before assuming a repair is a full out-of-pocket cost.

Preventing the Next Service Call

A whole-home filter ahead of the unit, or a softener if the household doesn't already have one, meaningfully slows the mineral buildup that drives most service calls on Westminster's harder water.

Keeping the area around the unit clear of stored items also matters more than people expect, since restricted airflow around a gas unit's intake can trigger error codes that have nothing to do with the water side at all.

What to Expect When You Call

Tell the dispatcher the unit's brand if you know it and whether it's showing an error code, since that alone often narrows down the likely cause before a technician even arrives.

A typical descale visit runs about an hour once the technician is on-site, and we'll walk through the unit's error history with you in plain language before recommending anything beyond the standard flush.

Serving Westminster and Nearby

We run tankless service calls across Westminster's newer construction, including the 80023 corridor near Broomfield and the 80234 area toward Northglenn, where on-demand units are more common than in the city's older core.

Signs You Shouldn't Ignore

  • Hot water takes longer to arrive than it used to
  • The unit displays an error code or flashing light
  • Water temperature swings while running
  • You can hear popping or rumbling from the unit
  • It's been 12+ months since the last flush

Common Questions

Tankless Water Heater Service FAQ

How often should a tankless unit be flushed in Westminster?

With water sourced largely through Clear Creek, most manufacturers and our own service history point to once a year for a typical household, closer to every 8 months if the home runs a water softener that isn't keeping up.

Can a tankless unit just be repaired instead of replaced?

Most of the calls we run are a repair, not a replacement. The heat exchanger itself is the expensive part, and it usually survives years past the point where a descale or a sensor swap would have solved the actual complaint.

Does a tankless unit really save money long-term?

It typically uses less energy than a tank since it's not maintaining a standing reservoir of hot water around the clock, though the higher upfront cost means the payback period is usually several years, not immediate.
