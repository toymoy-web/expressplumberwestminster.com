About Hyland Hills

Hyland Hills is the name carried by the park district and the golf courses around Westminster Center Park in southern Westminster, and we use it for the established residential area nearby. A real estate guide on homes.com labels that surrounding area Hyland Greens and places it in ZIP code 80031.

That guide describes about 3,600 homes with a median build year of 1977. It says the area was developed in the 1970s with a park-like design, that almost every street ends in a cul-de-sac, and that sidewalks connect homes to pools and pocket parks. Sheridan Boulevard runs along one edge. No formal boundary is published, so we describe the area by its anchor roads and parks.

Carroll Butts Park, run by the Hyland Hills Park and Recreation District and co-owned with the City of Westminster, has ball fields, trails, a pond and an aquatic center. Westminster Center Park is nearby.

A 1970s neighborhood, in practice

Ranch and raised-ranch homes are prominent here, along with contemporary designs and some condos. A house built in the 1970s is now roughly fifty years old. The age tells you when to ask questions, and it does not tell you what the answers are. Most homes this old have had at least one round of repairs, so the useful question is what has been replaced and what has not.

Four things are worth checking in any house of this age. The first is the date on the water heater label. The second is whether the shutoff valves under sinks and toilets still turn freely, because valves left alone for years can stick or weep when first moved. The third is the age of the toilet fill valves. The fourth is where the sewer cleanout sits.

What to have ready when you call

A few details make the first call faster. Note the brand and age on the water heater label, where the main shutoff is, which fixtures are affected and what has already been repaired. A photo of the label and of any visible leak helps, and so does a note about when the problem started.

Services Hyland Hills homeowners ask about

Tank water heaters commonly last somewhere around 8 to 12 years, so a unit that is rusting, leaking at the base or short on hot water is a good candidate for water heater repair or replacement. Running toilets and weak flushes usually trace to worn parts, and our toilet repair page explains what a fix involves.

Slow drains in more than one fixture call for drain cleaning, and a line that clogs repeatedly is worth a camera inspection so the fix matches the cause.

Know where your water shuts off

Find your main shutoff inside the house before something goes wrong, and test that it turns. The City of Westminster owns the water line from the main to the meter pit, and the customer owns the line from the meter into the home. The city also sends a Possible Leak Alert when a meter records continuous use for 24 hours straight, and its guidance says that pattern usually means a leak.

If you get that alert, check toilets and outdoor faucets first. If nothing obvious turns up, a leak locator can find a hidden source without opening walls.

Common Questions

Hyland Hills Plumbing FAQ

Is Hyland Hills the same as Hyland Greens?

They describe the same general area. Hyland Hills is the name carried by the park district and the golf courses, and Hyland Greens is the label a real estate guide uses for the surrounding neighborhood. Neither has a published boundary, so we go by the anchor roads and parks.

How do I know my water heater is near the end of its life?

Look at the label for the manufacture date. Tank units commonly last roughly 8 to 12 years, and rust at the base, moisture on the floor, rumbling noises and slow recovery are common warning signs. A plumber can check the anode, the valves and the connections to judge whether repair still makes sense.

My bathroom shutoff valve drips when I turn it. What should I do?

Do not force it. A valve that has not moved in years can weep once disturbed. Shut the main valve if water is running, then call so the valve can be replaced properly. Replacing a worn shutoff is usually a small job compared with the damage from a failed one.

Do you serve homes near Carroll Butts Park and the golf courses?

Yes, we take calls from across Westminster, including this area. Call (303) 552-3896, describe the problem and give the street. The dispatcher will explain what happens next.

What does a sewer camera inspection show?

The camera shows the inside of the line, including roots, cracks, offset joints, sagging sections and buildup. It lets the repair match the cause instead of treating the same clog again. Ask for the recording or photos so you have a record of what the line looked like on that day.
