North and Northwest Denver, Closest to Our Base

North and northwest Denver is the closest part of the city to our Westminster base, around ten minutes from the Berkeley and Regis neighborhoods by our estimate. This page covers that corner of Denver specifically, including Berkeley, Regis, Sunnyside, Highland, West Highland, Sloan's Lake, and Chaffee Park, not the whole city.

Many homes here are far older than anything in Westminster. Denver Water says homes built before 1951 are more likely to have lead service lines, and its Lead Reduction Program, which began in January 2020 as a 15-year effort, has been replacing them neighborhood by neighborhood across north Denver.

Denver Water, Lead Service Lines, and Who Owns the Pipe

Denver Water serves these neighborhoods, not a Westminster utility, and the customer owns the service line from the main to the home. That makes the utility's no-charge replacement program important to anyone with an older house, and it affects how we plan a repair near the meter.

Denver Water's guidance is that a leaking lead service line should be replaced in full with copper rather than patched, and the work needs permits from Denver Water and the local permit office. We coordinate that sequence instead of patching a line the utility would rather see replaced.

Sunnyside, Regis, Highland, and Berkeley

Sunnyside, Regis, Chaffee Park, and West Highland were among the first north Denver neighborhoods on the utility's service line replacement schedule, with Sloan's Lake, Highland, and the Federal Boulevard corridor following. After a service line is replaced, older interior plumbing sometimes shows its age, so a pressure check and a look at the main shutoff are a sensible follow-up.

Highland and Berkeley mix older houses with newer infill. The older houses often have cast iron or clay sewer laterals, and a camera inspection is the safest first step before any repair estimate.

Freeze-Thaw and Low Ground Near Sloan's Lake

North Denver shares the Front Range freeze-thaw cycle with the rest of our territory. Older homes with additions often have the most exposed supply lines, and a hard cold snap shows up first in uninsulated garages and crawl spaces.

The low ground around Sloan's Lake is worth a sump pump and drainage check before spring melt, especially for basements that have stayed damp in earlier seasons.

Where North Denver Fits in Our Coverage

Federal Heights, Sherrelwood, and Berkley are the small communities that sit between Westminster and the Denver line, which is why a north Denver call is a short drive for us and not a cross-metro trip. We do not claim every address in a city this size, and we say so on the phone.

Denver issues its own plumbing permits through the city, separate from Westminster, Adams County, or Jefferson County. We pull the right permit for the right jurisdiction.

All work in Denver follows the Colorado Plumbing Code (IPC) under DORA licensing, plus any Denver Water requirements for service line connections.

Denver Plumbing FAQ

Do you serve all of Denver?

We serve north and northwest Denver, including Berkeley, Regis, Sunnyside, Highland, West Highland, Sloan's Lake, and Chaffee Park, because that is the part of the city closest to our Westminster base. For addresses farther south, call and we will tell you honestly whether we can respond quickly.

Can you replace a lead service line?

Denver Water replaces customer-owned lead service lines with copper at no direct charge through its Lead Reduction Program, and many homeowners prefer to wait for the utility's schedule. If a leak forces the issue sooner, the utility's guidance is full replacement with copper, and we can walk you through the permit steps.

How can I tell if my home has a lead service line?

Denver Water says homes built before 1951 are more likely to have lead service lines, and it is building an inventory from property records, water tests, and visual inspections. You can contact the utility directly, or ask us to look at the pipe where it enters the house.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Denver?

We cover the north Denver ZIP codes closest to us: 80211 across Highland and Sunnyside, 80212 across Berkeley and Sloan's Lake, and 80221 around Regis University.

Is emergency service available in Denver?

Yes. Our 24/7 emergency line dispatches to every Denver ZIP code we cover, day or night, for a burst pipe, active leak, or sewer backup. Call (303) 552-3896 and a real dispatcher answers, not a voicemail.
