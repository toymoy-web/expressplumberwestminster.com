About Legacy Ridge

Legacy Ridge is a residential community in north Westminster organized around Legacy Ridge Golf Course. The City of Westminster operates the course at 10801 Legacy Ridge Parkway. It opened in 1994 and is described by the city as a Colorado-style Scottish links layout with mountain views, wetlands and prairie grasses.

A homes.com neighborhood guide places Legacy Ridge in ZIP code 80031 and counts about 1,900 homes with a median build year of 1995. It describes mostly single-family houses, including split-levels, two-story brick homes and large custom homes overlooking the course, with some townhouses and condos. West 112th Avenue and Legacy Ridge Parkway are the main roads named. Cotton Creek Park and the Big Dry Creek area sit nearby.

What larger 1990s homes change

The guide puts the average single-family home at roughly 2,800 square feet. Bigger homes tend to have more bathrooms, longer pipe runs and more fixtures drawing hot water at the same time. In a house like that, a long wait for hot water at a far bathroom is a common complaint, and a recirculation setup or a different water heater sizing may be worth discussing.

Our tankless water heater page covers how tankless units are sized and maintained, and the water heater replacement page covers the tank option. The right answer depends on the household, not on the subdivision name.

Hard water and fixtures

The City of Westminster's 2021 water quality report lists hardness at 113 to 128 mg/L as calcium carbonate, about 7 to 8 grains per gallon, and says the water begins as snowmelt from Clear Creek stored in Standley Lake. Numbers change by year, so check the latest report or test your own tap.

Mineral scale shows up on faucet aerators, showerheads and water heater elements. If you are deciding whether a softener makes sense, our water softener service page explains the options. For a faucet or toilet that has started to misbehave, toilet repair is a common first call.

Kitchens and bathrooms in larger homes

More bathrooms and a bigger kitchen mean more fixtures that wear. Garbage disposals jam, toilets run, shower valves drip and faucets lose flow to scale. Our garbage disposal page covers the common problems. When several fixtures act up together, ask whether water pressure or the water heater is involved before replacing parts one at a time.

Know who owns which water line

The City of Westminster owns the water line from the main to the meter pit, and the customer owns the line from the meter into the building. The city publishes a Service Line Inventory map where you can enter your address and click the dot in front of your home to see meter and service line information. If your line shows as unknown, the city asks residents to call 303-658-2462.

Townhomes and condos in the neighborhood

Some Legacy Ridge homes are attached, and in an attached home the line between your plumbing and the association's can be unclear. Before you call, check your association documents for who handles shared lines. A plumber can locate the source of a leak either way, and leak detection helps when the water is not where the damage shows.

Bradburn Village, a planned community along 120th Avenue, is listed as a nearby neighborhood. Its neighborhood page covers a different mix of housing.

Sources: City of Westminster, Legacy Ridge Golf Course, homes.com Legacy Ridge neighborhood guide (home counts, build years and sizes are estimates), and the City of Westminster 2021 Water Quality Report.

Common Questions

Legacy Ridge Plumbing FAQ

Is Legacy Ridge in Westminster or Broomfield?

The City of Westminster operates Legacy Ridge Golf Course at 10801 Legacy Ridge Parkway, and a real estate guide lists the neighborhood under a Westminster ZIP code, 80031. The neighborhood is in north Westminster, and a Broomfield community, Cimarron Village, is listed as a nearby neighborhood. Check your tax or utility bill if you are unsure which city serves your address.

Does a larger home need a tankless water heater?

Not automatically. Tankless units suit some households and tank units suit others. The right choice depends on how many people live in the home, how many fixtures run at once and how much space and venting are available. A plumber can walk through the numbers for your house.

Is Westminster water hard enough to need a softener?

The city's 2021 report lists hardness at 113 to 128 mg/L, which is about 7 to 8 grains per gallon. Whether a softener makes sense is a personal decision based on scale on fixtures, appliance life and cost. Test your tap or check the city's latest report before you decide.

A leak in my townhome might be from the unit next door. Who fixes it?

Check your association documents for who is responsible for shared lines. A plumber can find where the water is coming from, and that information helps the association or the other owner act. Shut off your own supply if water is running into your unit.

Hot water takes a long time to reach my far bathroom. What can I do?

Distance from the water heater is the usual reason, because the cooled water in the pipe has to run out first. Check the water heater's temperature setting and age, then ask about a recirculation loop if the wait is long. A plumber can tell you whether the fix is a setting, a part or a layout change.
