Privacy Policy

Westminster Express Plumbing · Westminster, CO

What Information We Collect

This website does not collect personal information through any online form, since we don't use forms for scheduling, quotes, or contact. Any personal information we have, your name, address, phone number, or details about a plumbing job, comes from a phone call you make to us directly, not from anything submitted through this site.

Like most websites, this site may use basic analytics tools (such as aggregate visit and page-view data) to understand how the site is used. This data is not tied to your name or contact information unless you've called us separately.

How We Use Information From Phone Calls

Information gathered during a phone call, your name, address, phone number, and details about the plumbing issue, is used solely to schedule, perform, and bill for the service you've requested. We do not sell or rent this information to third parties.

Calls may be recorded or monitored for quality and training purposes, consistent with standard business practice and applicable Colorado law.

Cookies

This site may use standard browser cookies for basic functionality and anonymous analytics. You can disable cookies through your browser settings. Doing so may affect some site functionality but will not affect your ability to call us.

Third-Party Links

This site may link to third-party services, such as a Google Maps embed. Those third parties have their own privacy practices, which we don't control and this policy doesn't cover.

Contact Us About Privacy

If you have a question about this privacy policy or how your information is handled, contact us using the phone number or email address listed on our Contact page.
