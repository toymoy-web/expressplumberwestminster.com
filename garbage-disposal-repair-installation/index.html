Humming, Jammed, or Leaking Disposals

A disposal that hums but doesn't spin is almost always jammed, not burned out, and usually clears with the reset button and a manual turn of the flywheel from underneath rather than a full replacement.

A disposal leaking from the bottom is a different problem, the seal has failed, and at that point repair isn't really an option since the unit's housing is sealed at the factory.

Repair or Replace

If a disposal jams repeatedly even after clearing it, or it's leaking from the housing itself, replacement is the more cost-effective call given how affordable a new unit is relative to the labor involved either way.

Choosing a Replacement Unit

Horsepower matters less than most homeowners assume for a typical household. A standard 1/2 to 3/4 horsepower unit handles normal kitchen waste fine. The step up to 1 horsepower mostly matters for households that regularly grind tougher scraps like corn cobs or fibrous vegetable peels.

We install whichever unit fits the household's actual use, rather than defaulting to the most expensive option available.

What Actually Causes Most Jams

Fibrous produce like celery and corn husks, along with potato peels, are the most common jam cause we see, more than bones or harder scraps that people usually assume are the problem.

Running cold water, not hot, while the disposal is on also helps solidify grease into small pieces that clear rather than coating the chamber, which is the opposite of common assumption.

A Fast, Straightforward Call

Most disposal calls are diagnosed within the first few minutes, since a jam versus a burned-out motor versus a leaking seal each present differently and don't require extended troubleshooting to tell apart.

A jam-clearing visit typically takes 20 to 30 minutes. A full replacement, including removing the old unit, usually runs about an hour.

Brands We Install and Service

We work with InSinkErator, Waste King, and most other major disposal brands sold at Denver-area retailers, and carry common replacement units on the truck for same-day swaps rather than special-ordering for every job.

If a specific brand's warranty is still active, we'll note that during the visit, since some manufacturers cover parts even when the labor itself falls to you.

Serving Westminster and Nearby

Disposal calls come from kitchens across the city, with a noticeable concentration in the newer builds near 80023 where garbage disposals are standard equipment.

We also check the dishwasher air-gap connection during any disposal visit, since a surprising number of disposal-adjacent leaks actually trace back to that fitting rather than the disposal unit itself.

We double-check the dishwasher drain connection and air gap during any disposal replacement too, since those fittings are easy to overlook and can cause a slow leak that shows up weeks after the main repair. We'll also flag if your existing outlet or switch wiring looks outdated, since older homes sometimes still run a disposal off a setup that wouldn't meet current code on a new install. That's a quick, no-charge check we do as a matter of course, not a separate billable inspection.

Signs You Shouldn't Ignore

  • Humming sound but blades won't spin
  • Leaking from underneath the sink
  • Unusually loud grinding or rattling
  • Disposal trips the breaker repeatedly
  • Won't turn on at all

Common Questions

Garbage Disposal Repair & Installation FAQ

Can a jammed disposal be fixed without replacing it?

Most jams clear with the hex-key reset most units have on the bottom, paired with the red reset button. If it hums but won't turn even after that, the motor itself has usually failed and replacement is the practical fix.

What shouldn't go down a garbage disposal?

Fibrous vegetables like celery, coffee grounds in large amounts, grease, and bones all cause more jams than people expect, even in a healthy, properly working unit.
