When Replacement Makes More Sense Than Another Repair

A tank past 10 to 12 years old that needs a second or third repair is usually past the point where repair is the better financial call, especially once the tank itself starts to rust from the inside rather than a part failing.

The blocks around Orchard Town Center and the newer development pushing north toward 144th Ave are recent enough that PEX and tankless systems are the norm, which shifts the most common service call from corrosion to electronics and descaling.

Tank vs. Tankless for Westminster Homes

Older sections of the city with existing gas tank setups usually stay tank-to-tank, since the venting and gas line are already sized correctly, which keeps the install straightforward.

Newer construction, or a household planning a remodel anyway, is where switching to tankless makes the most sense, since the rough-in for a larger gas line or dedicated venting is easier to do during other work than as a standalone retrofit.

What's Included

A full replacement includes removing and hauling away the old unit, code-correct venting and strapping, a new expansion tank where the local code requires one, and a pressure test before we leave.

We install all major brands, Rheem, A.O. Smith, Rinnai, and Navien among them, and price the unit separately from labor so you can see exactly what you're paying for.

Permits and Code Requirements

Westminster requires a permit for water heater replacement, and our installs are pulled and inspected to code, including the expansion tank requirement that trips up a lot of DIY or unlicensed installs.

Skipping the permit might save a small fee up front, but it becomes a real problem at resale, when a home inspector flags unpermitted work and a buyer's lender asks questions before closing.

Sizing the Right Unit for Your Household

A 40-gallon tank suits most 2 to 3 person households. A larger household or one with a soaking tub often needs 50 gallons or a tankless unit sized for simultaneous fixture use.

We size based on actual household habits, not just a generic per-person formula, since two households the same size can have very different hot water demand depending on routines.

Planning the Install Day

We confirm the unit type, fuel source, and any code items (like an expansion tank) during the initial quote, so installation day itself doesn't turn up surprises that change the price.

Hot water is typically restored within a few hours of the crew arriving for a standard tank swap, and we test for leaks and proper temperature before considering the job finished.

Serving Westminster and Nearby

Replacements happen citywide, from 80020 and 80021 near Broomfield down through 80260 toward Federal Heights.

We register the new unit's warranty on your behalf where the manufacturer allows it, one less step you have to remember after installation day wraps up.

Signs You Shouldn't Ignore

  • Tank is over 10 years old
  • Second or third repair on the same unit
  • Rust-colored water from the hot side only
  • Water heater is undersized for the household
  • Planning a remodel that touches the water heater closet

Common Questions

Water Heater Installation & Replacement FAQ

How long does a replacement take?

A straight tank-to-tank swap is usually a same-day job, typically 2 to 3 hours. A tankless conversion that needs new gas line sizing or venting can run a full day depending on the route.

Do you haul away the old unit?

Yes, haul-away is included on every replacement, and we recycle the old tank rather than sending it to a landfill where the local facility allows it.

Can you install a water heater the same day you quote it?

For a straightforward tank-to-tank swap with the unit in stock, yes, same-day installation is usually possible. A tankless conversion needing gas line or venting work is more often scheduled a day or two out.
