When It's Not a Big Job, Just an Annoying One

Not every plumbing problem is a burst pipe or a dead water heater. A dripping faucet, a running toilet, a loose fixture, or a slow leak under a sink are all real problems worth fixing before they get worse or waste water month after month.

We run general repair calls the same day or next day in most cases, since these jobs don't carry the same urgency as an active leak but shouldn't sit for weeks either.

What Falls Under General Repair

Faucet and fixture repair, running or leaking toilets, minor pipe leaks, shutoff valve replacement, water pressure troubleshooting, and any plumbing issue that doesn't fit neatly into a bigger category.

When a Small Job Turns Into a Bigger One

A minor leak under a sink can look simple and turn out to be a corroded shutoff valve that needs replacing rather than just a tightened fitting, which is why we quote the actual repair on-site rather than sight-unseen over the phone.

We'll always tell you plainly if a job that looked small on the phone turns out to need more work once we're actually looking at it, before doing anything beyond what was quoted.

Bundling Small Repairs Into One Visit

If you've got a running toilet, a slow drain, and a dripping faucet all sitting on a mental list, it's almost always more cost-effective to bundle them into one visit than call separately for each.

We'll walk through the house with you if asked, rather than fixing only the one item on the original call, so small stuff doesn't keep accumulating.

What a Typical Visit Looks Like

We diagnose on arrival and quote the actual repair before starting, rather than a phone estimate that can shift once we're looking at the real fixture or pipe involved.

Most general repair calls, a faucet, a running toilet, a minor leak, are completed within an hour of the technician arriving, parts and labor both included in the quoted price.

Fixtures and Areas We Cover

Kitchens, bathrooms, laundry connections, and utility areas all fall under general repair, whether it's a leaking supply line to a washing machine or a dripping outdoor spigot that's been ignored through a few seasons.

If something in the house uses water and isn't working quite right, it's worth a call even if it doesn't fit neatly into one of our other named service categories.

Serving Westminster and Nearby

General repair calls come from every ZIP we cover, 80003 through 80260, with no travel surcharge added within the city.

We're also glad to do a quick whole-house walkthrough on request, flagging anything worth keeping an eye on even if it isn't the reason for today's visit, at no extra charge beyond the service call.

We keep a simple record of what we've serviced at your address, so a future call, even a different technician, has real context on the home's plumbing history rather than starting from zero.

Signs You Shouldn't Ignore

  • A dripping faucet that's gotten worse
  • Loose or wobbly fixtures
  • Low water pressure at one fixture
  • A shutoff valve that won't fully close
  • Minor leaks under a sink or behind a toilet

Common Questions

General Plumbing Repair FAQ

What counts as an emergency vs. general repair?

Active flooding, a burst pipe, or a sewer backup are emergencies. A slow drip, a running toilet, or a loose fixture can typically wait for a scheduled visit, which usually costs less than after-hours dispatch.

Do you charge a trip fee just to look at something?

Our diagnostic visit is free when paired with the repair. If you decide not to move forward after the diagnosis, we're upfront about that separately before the visit.
