Licensed, Call-Only, Upfront Pricing

Westminster Express Plumbing is a licensed plumbing service operating under Colorado's DORA-administered Plumbing Code, which incorporates the International Plumbing Code (IPC) with state-specific amendments. Every technician we dispatch works to that same statewide standard, whether the job is a five-minute faucet repair or a full whole-home repipe.

We run a call-only model. There's no online quote form on this site, no automated estimate generator, and no request queue you drop into and wait on. You call, a real dispatcher answers, and either a technician is on the way or you have a scheduled appointment before you hang up.

What Upfront Pricing Actually Means Here

A flat price is confirmed by phone or on-site before any repair work begins, not estimated loosely and adjusted once a technician has already started. Non-emergency work gets a free estimate. Emergency dispatch carries a confirmed price agreed to before the truck rolls, not after.

We don't advertise a number we can't stand behind once we're actually looking at the job. If a quoted price changes because the scope changes once we're on-site, we explain exactly why before doing any additional work, not after the invoice arrives.

Where We Work

Our primary service area is Westminster, Colorado, spanning both Adams and Jefferson counties, plus the surrounding communities of Broomfield, Arvada, Thornton, Northglenn, and Federal Heights. Each of those cities has its own real housing history and plumbing profile, which is part of why we treat every estimate individually rather than pricing a job the same regardless of where it is or how old the home's original plumbing happens to be.

That local variation matters more than it might seem. A home in Arvada's Olde Town historic district and a newer build near Broomfield's Anthem Highlands can need entirely different diagnostic approaches even for the same reported symptom, and knowing that going in changes what a technician brings on the first visit.

What We Don't Do

We don't fabricate reviews, invent a founding year, or claim certifications we don't actually hold. If you ask us directly about a specific credential and we don't have it, we'll tell you plainly rather than talk around the question.

We also don't claim a guaranteed arrival window we can't reliably honor. Response times vary with current call volume and conditions, and we'd rather be honest about that upfront than promise something we might not deliver.

We don't upsell a full replacement when a repair genuinely is the better call, and we don't quote a repair when a system has clearly reached the point where replacement is the more honest recommendation. The diagnosis drives the recommendation, not the other way around.

How We Handle Every Call

Every call gets a real diagnosis before a repair recommendation, not a default upsell to the most expensive option available. We explain what we found, what the real options are, and what each one costs, and we let you make the decision with actual information rather than pressure.

For emergency calls specifically, we prioritize genuinely urgent situations, active flooding, sewer backups, gas concerns, ahead of scheduled work, and we're upfront on the phone about whether what you're describing needs a truck tonight or can reasonably wait for a standard appointment.

For non-emergency work, we're glad to walk through timing that fits your schedule rather than defaulting to whatever's most convenient for our own routing. A running toilet or a slow drain doesn't need to disrupt your day the way an actual emergency does.

Why We Publish Real Numbers

Every price range on this site reflects typical costs for the actual jobs we run in this specific market, not a national average pulled from somewhere else. We'd rather you walk into a call with a realistic sense of what to expect than be surprised by a number that has nothing to do with what similar jobs actually cost here.

That same standard applies to the local research throughout this site, real neighborhoods, real landmarks, real housing history for each city we serve. If a detail wasn't verifiable, it isn't on this site, even where a generic-sounding claim might have read just as convincingly.

Questions Before You Call

If you're comparing plumbers and want to verify a license before committing to anyone, Colorado's DORA maintains a public license lookup, and we'd encourage you to use it regardless of who you ultimately hire. A legitimate, licensed plumber will never hesitate to have that checked.

If you're not sure whether your situation is an emergency or something that can wait for a scheduled visit, that's a completely reasonable question to lead with when you call. We'd rather talk through it honestly than have you either overpay for after-hours dispatch on something that could wait, or delay on something that genuinely shouldn't.
