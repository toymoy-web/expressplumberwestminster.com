Westminster's Freeze-Thaw Pattern Is the Real Culprit

It's rarely a single brutally cold night that cracks a pipe. It's the repeated freeze-thaw cycle common to the Front Range, where a pipe freezes, expands, and cracks internally, then the thaw that follows is what actually reveals the leak once water starts flowing through the damaged section again.

Exposed pipe in garages, crawl spaces, and unheated additions is the most common location, since these areas don't get the ambient heat that protects pipe running through conditioned living space.

What To Do the Moment You Suspect a Frozen Pipe

If a faucet has reduced or no flow during a cold snap, shut off the main water supply to the affected area if possible, open the faucet slightly to relieve pressure, and call before attempting to thaw it yourself with an open flame, which is a real fire risk in tight spaces.

We treat suspected frozen pipe as same-day emergency work, not a scheduled visit, since a pipe that's already cracked internally can start leaking heavily the moment it thaws. Any pipe replacement is completed to the Colorado Plumbing Code (IPC), not just patched to get water flowing again.

Which Pipes Are Actually at Risk

Pipe running through an uninsulated crawl space, an attached garage, or an exterior wall without adequate insulation is what actually freezes in a Front Range winter. Pipe inside conditioned living space rarely has a problem even during a hard freeze.

A quick walkthrough before winter, checking exposed pipe in these specific areas, catches most of the risk before it becomes an emergency call during the coldest week of the year.

Preparing for Next Winter Now

Insulating exposed pipe, sealing drafts near crawl space vents, and knowing where the main shutoff is are the three most effective, lowest-cost steps a Westminster homeowner can take before the first hard freeze of the season.

We're glad to do a quick pre-winter walkthrough focused specifically on freeze risk, separate from a general plumbing inspection, if you'd rather have it checked than guess.

If You Suspect a Frozen Pipe Right Now

Shut off the main if you can access it, open the affected faucet slightly, and avoid any open-flame thawing method, then call rather than waiting to see if it resolves on its own.

We treat suspected frozen pipe as same-day priority work throughout the Front Range winter, since the moment of thaw is often when a hidden crack starts actively leaking.

Serving Westminster and Nearby

Frozen pipe calls spike every winter across Westminster, with older homes near 80030 and 80221 and their less-insulated crawl spaces seeing the earliest calls of the season.

We also check attic and crawl space insulation levels during a frozen pipe call, since a gap in insulation is often the root cause behind why one specific pipe froze while the rest of the home's plumbing was fine.

Signs You Shouldn't Ignore

  • No water or reduced flow from a faucet during cold weather
  • Frost visible on an exposed pipe
  • A bulging section of visible pipe
  • Water flowing after a thaw following a hard freeze
  • A previously reliable line failing only in winter

Common Questions

Frozen Pipe Repair FAQ

How can I prevent frozen pipes next winter?

Insulating exposed pipe in garages and crawl spaces, disconnecting outdoor hoses before the first hard freeze, and keeping cabinet doors open under sinks on exterior walls during a cold snap all meaningfully reduce the risk in a Front Range winter.

Is it safe to thaw a pipe myself?

A hair dryer or heat tape on an accessible section is generally safe. An open flame near a pipe, especially inside a wall or tight crawl space, is a real fire hazard and not something we'd recommend without a professional there.

Can pipe insulation actually prevent a freeze?

Foam pipe insulation on exposed lines meaningfully reduces freeze risk during a typical Front Range cold snap, though it's not a guarantee during an extended hard freeze without any heat source nearby.
